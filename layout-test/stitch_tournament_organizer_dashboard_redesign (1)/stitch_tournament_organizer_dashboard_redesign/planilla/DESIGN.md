## Brand & Style
The design system embodies the high-stakes precision, visual authority, and refined kinetic elegance of elite international tournament administration and athletic broadcast editorial. It merges the institutional clarity of UEFA/FIFA competition control centers with the polished, metric-driven minimalism of the Nike Athletic Director suite and Strava’s clean performance interfaces.

### Aesthetic Movement & Personality
- **Style Archetype:** Modern Athletic Editorial & High-Density Minimalist.
- **Personality Attributes:** Decisive, authoritative, hyper-legible, prestigious, and performance-tuned.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires competitive confidence, instant operational clarity, and the crisp gravitas of world-class sporting events.

Visual surfaces prioritize luminous pure white canvases framed by razor-sharp slate borders, hyper-condensed display metrics, and decisive athletic blue focal points. The environment rejects superfluous skeuomorphic visual noise in favor of broadcast-grade hierarchy, typographic density, and surgical data alignment.

## Colors
This design system operates exclusively in a pristine, daylight-tuned light mode engineered for extreme contrast, broadcast readability, and immediate comprehension under rapid operational conditions.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1d4ed8` - Electric Royal Indigo):** Anchors primary tournament actions, focal interactions, active navigation states, and selected roster metrics. Conveys authoritative athleticism.
- **Secondary (`#0f172a` - Deep Stadium Slate):** Serves as the bedrock for high-contrast headlines, primary labels, dark badges, and high-impact structural anchors. 
- **Secondary Sub-slate (`#1e293b` & `#334155`):** Reserved for secondary body text, table row dividers, and contextual editorial bylines.
- **Tertiary (`#059669` - Championship Emerald):** Deployed selectively for real-time match events, active live timers, confirmed bracket advances, and verified team compliance.
- **Neutral Accent & Borders (`#e2e8f0` / `#cbd5e1`):** Precision titanium divider tones that maintain distinct separation across dense matrix data without heavy visual mass.
- **Surface Canvas (`#ffffff`):** Pure white canvas complemented by `#f8fafc` for secondary column wells, alternating match-sheet rows, and scoreboard sub-panels.

## Typography
Typography reflects high-octane broadcast presentation balanced with systematic administrative rigour. 

- **Chivo** commands all primary headings, bracket group labels, match titles, and aggregate scores. Its technical geometric cuts evoke athletic authority, modern track timers, and editorial power.
- **Hanken Grotesk** serves as the neutral, high-clarity workhorse for rosters, competition logs, analytical reports, and management controls.
- **JetBrains Mono** is deployed strictly for tabular timekeeping, live match clocks (e.g., `89:42 +3'`), jersey numerals, goal tallies, and precision tracking metrics, preventing optical shifting during live score updates.

### Usage Directives
- Apply uppercase styling with deliberate tracking (`0.05em` to `0.08em`) to small metadata headers, section tickers, and stadium badge tags.
- Score counts and aggregate totals must use tabular numeral spacing to maintain rigid vertical axis alignment in fixture lists.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a precision 12-column dynamic grid designed for both panoramic wide-screen command hubs (desktop) and compact, touch-optimized tournament tracking (mobile).

### Layout System
- **Grid Structure:** 12-column layout with 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters on desktop screens scaling down to a single-column or 4-column reflow with 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters on mobile viewports.
- **Canvas Bounds:** Max container width tops out at 1440px for editorial bracket structures; administrative data tables leverage a fluid edge-to-edge layout capped with 32px canvas margins.
- **Data Density:** High spatial economy across tabular feeds and scoreboards. Match cells feature compact vertical spacing (`space-sm` to `space-md`), minimizing unnecessary scroll overhead while isolating actionable elements with crisp structural divider lines.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation in this system eschews heavy atmospheric blurs in favor of crisp architectural separation, subtle cold-tinted layering, and hyper-subtle titanium borders.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Pitch/Canvas Base):** Solid `#ffffff` background with optional microscopic high-density hairline grid structures for visual alignment.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Modular Panels):** Crisp white surface surrounded by a 1px solid border (`#e2e8f0`) and reinforced by a faint athletic shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active Fixtures):** Score overlays, active flyouts, and live popovers utilize an athletic elevated shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` combined with a 1px border (`#cbd5e1`).
4. **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Match Center Takeovers):** Solid white containers framed by high-contrast slate scrims (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.40)`) with crisp `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

## Shapes
Shapes communicate precision, discipline, and athletic geometry. 

- UI elements adhere to a curated **Soft** profile (base radius: `0.25rem` / `4px`), ensuring cards, fixture blocks, table segments, and input fields remain architecturally structured and sharp.
- High-level containers, modals, and stadium overview panels scale gracefully up to `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`).
- Real-time status indicators, player badge tags, and match phase tickers utilize fully rounded pill contours (`9999px`) to create an immediate visual counterpoint against the rectangular grid structure.

## Components

### Action Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid electric royal indigo background (`#1d4ed8`), text `#ffffff`, font `Hanken Grotesk` 700. In hover state, transitions to `#1e40af` with an energetic 1px micro-drop shadow. Sharp 4px corner radius.
- **Secondary Action:** Stadium slate container (`#0f172a`), text `#ffffff`. Used for executive bracket locks, export controls, and final confirmation events.
- **Outline / Editorial:** Pristine white background, 1px border (`#cbd5e1`), text `#0f172a`. Hover transitions border to `#1d4ed8` and background to `#f8fafc`.

### Scoreboard Fixture Cards & Match Rows
- **Card Shell:** Pure white card bounded by `border-slate-200`, `rounded` (4px). Header features a 28px slate metadata strip indicating match phase, stadium venue, and competition group.
- **Score Matrix:** Split team layout featuring mono-spaced bold numbers (`JetBrains Mono`) centered in light slate wells (`#f1f5f9`). Winning score displays in `#0f172a` with bold styling; trailing score displays in `#64748b`.
- **Live Match Indicator:** Pill badge with `#ecfdf5` background, `#059669` text, and a pulsing 6px emerald status circle positioned adjacent to the live match clock.

### Chips & Pill Badges
- **Status Pills:** Compact, uppercase `11px` typography with `0.06em` letter spacing. 
  - *Active / In-Play:* Green field (`#ecfdf5`), `#047857` border, emerald typography.
  - *Upcoming / Staged:* Slate tint (`#f8fafc`), `#e2e8f0` border, `#475569` typography.
  - *Penalty / Disciplinary:* Amber (`#fffbeb`) or Crimson (`#fef2f2`) with respective borders for cards and official warnings.

### Data Tables & Roster Lists
- **Header:** Sticky table header with `#f8fafc` background, micro titanium bottom border (`#cbd5e1`), and uppercase tracking labels.
- **Rows:** Alternating subtle row shading (`#ffffff` to `#fcfdfe`), 40px row height for optimal tournament density, and subtle hover state (`#f1f5f9`). Cell dividers use hairline border `#f1f5f9`.

### Form Controls & Filter Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Pristine white background, 1px `#cbd5e1` border, 4px corner radius, internal padding `space-sm space-md`. Active focus state triggers an athletic 2px electric royal indigo ring offset with pure white padding.
- **Segmented Range / Stage Switches:** Slate container tray (`#f1f5f9`) hosting crisp elevated white toggle segments with 2px padding, casting subtle micro-shadows upon active selection.
